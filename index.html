Built from a page of a chess journal — first abstraction, vision, cover, geometry, information / compression / symbol, borders, symmetries — each turned into a layer you can switch on. Control counts every attack, including through the piece it lands on; the frontier is the zero contour of white attacks minus black attacks. Tension is weighted by the material standing in each contact, and material at risk is the full exchange played out cheapest-piece-first, with batteries uncovered as attackers step off — both read in pawns.
