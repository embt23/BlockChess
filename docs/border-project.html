Because you played yourself

One mind chose both sides

So any preference shows up twice in the same game. Compare the geometry of your White choices against your Black choices — same frontier lengths, same tension levels, same moment of commitment?

The hypothesis worth testing

A player against themselves steers toward positions they find legible. If that holds, the gaps aren't random — they're exactly the positions you avoid pushing either side into. That is invisible in a game against an opponent.
