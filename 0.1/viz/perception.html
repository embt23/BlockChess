is that discovery, or coincidence?

Share of discovered patterns that humans had already named, against a control of real openings people actually play. Below four plies the agreement is worse than chance. Above it, the compressor picks named sequences 3–6× more often than the pool it drew from — and coincidence gets weaker with length, not stronger.
